# Meridian Rail — Operations

## Service patterns

The core service runs every twelve minutes between Harbour Street and
Westmoor Halt from 05:30 to 23:30 on weekdays. The Marchlands branch is served
half-hourly, with alternate services terminating at Ferndown Junction. Sunday
service begins at 07:00 and runs at twenty-minute intervals on the core route.

The last departure from Harbour Street is at 23:42 on weekdays and 23:12 on
Sundays. There is no scheduled night service.

## Delay classification

A service is recorded as *on time* if it departs within fifty-nine seconds of
its scheduled time. A delay of between one and five minutes is classified as
minor and is not individually reported. A delay of six to fifteen minutes is
classified as significant and must be logged with a cause code. A delay
exceeding fifteen minutes, or a cancellation, is classified as a major
disruption and triggers the incident procedure below.

Cause codes are grouped into four families: infrastructure, rolling stock,
external, and operator. A delay whose cause has not been established within
twenty-four hours is coded as unexplained and reviewed weekly.

## Incident procedure

On a major disruption the duty controller must, in order: confirm the safety
of passengers and crew; notify the control centre at Callow Bridge; publish a
passenger announcement within six minutes; and open an incident record.

The incident record must be opened before any replacement service is
dispatched, so that the decision and its timing are captured. An incident
record that is opened after the fact is marked as retrospective and excluded
from punctuality reporting.

Replacement road transport may be authorised by the duty controller alone for
a disruption expected to last under ninety minutes. Beyond ninety minutes,
authorisation from the operations manager on call is required.

## Passenger compensation

Compensation is paid on a delay of thirty minutes or more at the passenger's
destination. The entitlement is 50 per cent of the single fare for a delay of
thirty to fifty-nine minutes, and 100 per cent for a delay of sixty minutes or
more. Holders of a Monthly Pass claim on a pro-rata daily value.

Claims must be submitted within twenty-eight days of the affected journey.
Compensation is not paid where the disruption was caused by an event outside
the operator's control and the operator published advance notice at least two
hours before the scheduled departure.
