# Meridian Rail — Ticketing and Fares

## Fare zones

The Meridian Rail network is divided into three fare zones. Zone 1 covers the
central district between Harbour Street and Callow Bridge. Zone 2 covers the
inner suburbs as far as Pellbrook and Westmoor Halt. Zone 3 covers the outer
network, including Ainsley, Ferndown Junction and the Marchlands branch.

There is no Zone 4. Services continuing beyond Marchlands are operated by
Northline and are not covered by Meridian Rail ticketing.

## Standard single fares

A standard single within Zone 1 costs 2.40. A single crossing two adjacent
zones costs 3.90. A single spanning all three zones costs 5.60. Children aged
five to fifteen travel at half the standard single fare, rounded down to the
nearest ten. Children under five travel free when accompanied by a fare-paying
passenger.

## Day and period tickets

The All-Zone Day Ticket costs 11.20 and is valid from first service until
04:00 the following morning. The Zone 1–2 Day Ticket costs 7.50 and is valid
on the same basis. A Monthly Pass costs 24 times the relevant Day Ticket price
and is valid for one calendar month from the date of first use.

Period tickets are not transferable between passengers. A period ticket
presented by someone other than the registered holder is treated as an invalid
ticket.

## Penalty fares

A passenger travelling without a valid ticket is liable for a penalty fare of
60.00, or twenty times the full single fare for the journey being made,
whichever is greater. The penalty fare is reduced to 30.00 if paid within
twenty-one days.

A passenger who holds a valid ticket but cannot produce it at the time of
inspection may apply within fourteen days for the penalty to be cancelled, on
production of the ticket and proof of purchase. An administration charge of
10.00 applies to a cancellation under this provision.

Penalty fares are not issued to passengers who boarded at a station where no
ticket office was open and no working ticket machine was available. Inspectors
must record the station and time in such cases.

## Refunds

An unused single or day ticket may be refunded within twenty-eight days of
purchase, less an administration charge of 2.50. Period tickets may be
refunded pro rata from the date the refund is requested, less the same charge.
Tickets bought at a discount under a promotional fare are not refundable.
